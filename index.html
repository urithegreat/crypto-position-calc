Dynamic RRR & Leverage Position Sizing

Steps from your Trading Journal:

  1. Place the amount of your capital in dollars into the designated "Capital ($)" input.
  2. Choose or input your "Risk in %" per trade using the input or quick-select buttons.
  3. Go to your chart using the Long/Short RRR tool in TradingView and identify your Stop Loss % and Take Profit %.
  4. Input your Stop Loss % into the designated "Stop Loss %" field (e.g., 0.85 for 0.85%).
  5. The calculator automatically computes the Suggested Margin to use across every leverage tier.
